FITFORM

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

In short: FitForm has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What you enter

To build your training plan, FitForm asks for an optional name, your sex, age, body weight, training goal, experience, training days and preferred equipment. As you train, you can record which sets you completed and the weights you used.

Where it is stored

All of this information is stored only on your device, in the app's local browser storage. It is never sent to us or to any server, and we cannot see it. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than showing you your plan and progress inside the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete a single profile from Profile → Edit → Delete profile, or erase everything from Profile → Privacy → Erase all my data on this device. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data in your browser settings also removes all stored information.

Permissions

FitForm does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or any health sensors.

Third-party services

  • Hosting: the app's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver files and keep the service secure. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • YouTube: each exercise has a "Watch on YouTube" button. Tapping it opens YouTube, which is governed by Google's own privacy policy. FitForm sends no data to YouTube.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries.

Health information

FitForm provides general fitness information and exercise demonstrations. It is not medical advice. Consult a doctor or another qualified healthcare professional before starting a new exercise program, especially if you have a medical condition, an injury, or are pregnant or recently gave birth.

Children

FitForm is intended for people aged 14 and over. Users under 18 should train with the guidance of a parent, guardian or qualified coach. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, email Tamreen Apps at tamreen.apps@gmail.com. If you email us, we only see what you choose to send, and we use it only to reply to you.
